The Everyday Reality

Why Prior Authorization Is Broken Today

Prior authorization is the paperwork insurance companies require before they pay for life-saving treatments. Today, that system is drowning doctors and endangering patients.

The Old Way (Manual & Unreliable)

Doctors and nurses spend up to 14 hours every single week filling out faxes, navigating 200+ conflicting insurance rulebooks, and sitting on telephone hold.

  • ✕ 7 to 14 Days Waiting: Cancer patients wait in agonizing suspense for treatment approvals.
  • ✕ The Black-Box Risk: Generic AI chatbots guess answers and cause wrongful insurance denials.
  • ✕ $180 Per Appeal: Hospitals waste millions fighting unjustified insurance rejections.
Result: Clinician burnout, delayed patient care, and massive financial waste.

The Prior Authorization Copilot Way

Our autonomous copilot extracts the clinical evidence, matches insurance guidelines in seconds, and prepares a complete packet for doctor approval.

  • ✔ Decisions in 4 Hours: Patients get their medications and surgeries on time, without delays.
  • ✔ 100% Doctor Controlled: A real physician must review and approve before anything is submitted.
  • ✔ Unbreakable Safety Net: Powered by AgenticLens & AgenticChaos to prevent software errors.
Result: Happy doctors, reassured patients, and fully compliant hospital revenue.

How It Works in 4 Simple Steps

No complex coding or jargon. Here is how your medical clinic processes an insurance approval from start to finish.

1

Patient Chart Arrives

The doctor enters clinical notes, patient history, and biopsy reports into the hospital system.

Input: Doctor's note & lab results
2

Evidence Extracted

The AI scans the document and accurately identifies the diagnosis, required lab numbers, and past treatments.

Check: Medical facts verified
3

Insurance Matched

The system cross-checks the patient’s exact records against insurance rules (Aetna, Blue Cross, UnitedHealthcare).

Rules: Coverage verified
4

Doctor 1-Click Sign-Off

A real doctor reviews the complete evidence packet, signs off in 1 click, and sends it directly to insurance.

Outcome: Approved for treatment!
